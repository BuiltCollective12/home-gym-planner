# GymPlanner — launch marketing playbook

Ranked. Do them top to bottom. Everything in tiers 1–3 is free.

---

## Read this first — two rules that protect the money

**1. Never post an Amazon affiliate link anywhere except gymplanner.org.**

Post `gymplanner.org`. Never a link with `tag=gymplanner-20` in it.

Reddit, Facebook groups and most forums ban affiliate links outright — you get
banned and the post gets deleted. Worse, Amazon only credits traffic from
channels you declared when you applied. A raw affiliate link dropped in a
comment is the fastest way to lose the account before it earns anything.

Sending people to the site is strictly better anyway: they build a plan, add
six things instead of one, and *then* hit your tagged cart link. Your commission
per visitor is many times higher.

**2. One real account per platform. No burners, no fake upvotes.**

Reddit's spam filter auto-removes links from accounts with no history — you'd
be posting into a void and wouldn't even know. Vote manipulation gets the
domain permanently banned sitewide, which would kill your best channel forever.
Use your real account. If you don't have one, make one and spend a week
commenting before you ever post a link.

**The clock that matters:** Amazon closes your account if you don't get
**3 qualifying sales in 180 days**. Three. That is the whole first goal — not
revenue, just three sales. Tier 1 alone should clear it.

# TIER 1 — Do today. Free, highest intent.

## 1. Reddit: answer "will this fit?" posts with a real plan link

This is your single best tactic and nothing else is close.

Your site encodes the whole plan in the URL. That means when someone posts
*"12x20 garage, will a rack and a treadmill fit?"* you can **build their exact
room, drag in their exact gear, copy the URL, and hand it to them.** That is not
an ad. That is the most useful reply in the thread.

**Where** (check each sub's rules before your first post):

- `r/homegym` — the big one, most active layout questions
- `r/GarageGym`
- `r/HomeGymPorn`
- `r/Fitness` — only inside its daily simple-questions thread
- `r/crossfit`, `r/powerlifting`, `r/weightroom` — occasional space questions
- `r/Garage`, `r/Basements`, `r/HomeImprovement` — people planning conversions

**How to find the posts:** on each sub, sort by New, and search these terms
weekly — `will this fit`, `layout`, `10x10`, `12x20`, `garage gym`,
`ceiling height`, `low ceiling`, `space`.

**The reply template.** Don't paste this verbatim every time — vary it, and
always mention something specific from their post or it reads as a bot:

> Threw your dimensions into a layout tool real quick — here's your room with
> the rack, bench and 6ft of platform: [link]
>
> You've got about 30" of walk-through on the left which is tight but doable.
> The thing I'd watch is ceiling height — a rack with a pull-up bar wants
> around 9ft to press overhead comfortably, and at 8ft you'll be ducking. You
> can drag stuff around in there if you want to try other arrangements.

**Rules that will get you removed if you ignore them:**

- Do not link the site in your *first ever* comment on a sub. Build 2–3 weeks
  of ordinary comment history first. Genuinely participate.
- Never link it in a top-level post on `r/homegym` without messaging the mods.
- Ratio: roughly 1 link for every 10 ordinary comments. If every comment you
  make has a link, you're a spammer and the mods will treat you as one.
- If someone asks "is this your site?" — **say yes.** Immediately and plainly.
  Reddit forgives a builder promoting their own free tool. It does not forgive
  being caught hiding it.

## 3. Pinterest — the most underrated channel for this exact niche

"Home gym layout" is a *visual planning* search, and Pinterest is where people
plan rooms. Pins keep pulling traffic for years, unlike a Reddit post that dies
in 48 hours. Free.

**Setup:** create a **Business** account (not personal — you need the
analytics), then claim gymplanner.org under Settings → Claimed accounts.

**Boards to make:**

- Garage Gym Layouts
- Small Home Gym Ideas
- Basement Gym Setups
- Home Gym Equipment Guides

**What to pin:** screenshot the 3D view of each of your four bundles, plus
layouts you build for common room sizes. Vertical images, 1000×1500px.

**Title and description formula** — Pinterest is a search engine, so write for
search:

> **Title:** 12x20 Garage Gym Layout — Rack, Bench & Cardio That Actually Fits
>
> **Description:** Full layout for a single-car garage: power rack, adjustable
> bench, 160lb bumper set and rubber flooring, with walking clearance kept at
> 30 inches. Built with a free planner where you can enter your own room size
> and drag equipment around in 3D before you buy anything. #homegym
> #garagegym #homegymideas #garagegymlayout

Pin 3–5 a day for the first two weeks, then a few a week. Link every pin to
gymplanner.org.

## 4. Short-form video: the planner *is* the content

Screen-record yourself building a gym in 30 seconds. Empty room → drag in rack,
bench, plates, flooring → flip to 3D → orbit the camera. That satisfying
before/after is exactly what the algorithm pushes.

Post the **same video** to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels. Free.

**Hooks** (say it in the first 2 seconds, and put it on screen as text):

- "Your garage gym won't fit. Here's how to check before you spend $3,000."
- "Building a $1,200 home gym in a 10x10 room"
- "POV: you bought a rack without measuring the ceiling"
- "What $1,200 vs $5,000 vs $10,000 actually looks like in the same garage"

That last one is your best video — three builds, same room, escalating budget.

**Caption:** `Free planner, link in bio. Enter your room size and drag stuff
around before you buy. #homegym #garagegym #homegymsetup`

Put `gymplanner.org` in the bio of all three profiles. TikTok and Instagram
strip links out of captions; the bio is the only place that works.

## 5. Facebook groups

Same rules as Reddit: join with your real account, comment for a week, then
help people with actual layouts.

Search Facebook for and join: **Home Gym Builders**, **Garage Gym Life**,
**Home Gym Equipment Buy/Sell/Trade**, **Budget Home Gyms**. There are several
large ones under each name — join 5–6.

These groups are *full* of "here's my space, what should I buy" posts with
photos. Reply with a built layout link. Many are more promotion-tolerant than
Reddit, but read the pinned rules post first — some ban links on sight and
remove you permanently.

# TIER 3 — Paid. Only after Tier 1 proves the site converts.

**Do not spend a dollar until you have your 3 qualifying sales.** You need to
know your conversion rate before you can know what a click is worth. Right now
you'd be guessing, and at 3% commission the margins are thin enough that
guessing loses money.

Rough math to hold in your head: a $2,000 gym at 3% pays you **$60**. If 1 in
50 visitors buys, a visitor is worth about **$1.20**. So any ad costing much
more than **$0.50 a click** is probably underwater once you account for people
who bounce.

Ranked by how well they fit this niche:

| Channel | Budget to test | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Reddit Ads** | $5–10/day | You can target `r/homegym` directly. Cheapest clicks of anything here and the exact audience. Start here. |
| **Pinterest Ads** | $5/day | Promote whichever organic pin already performed. Cheap, and the intent is "I am planning a room". |
| **Meta (FB/IG)** | $10/day | Best targeting, good home for the 3D video. Pricier, and needs the pixel installed. |
| **Google Search Ads** | $10/day | Highest intent ("home gym layout planner") but fitness keywords are expensive. Test last. |

For all of them: send traffic to `/planner`, not the homepage. The planner is
what converts; the homepage is a speed bump.

## 9. Cheap paid extras worth trying

- **Reddit ads on `r/homegym`** — genuinely the best $50 test available to you.
- **Sponsoring a small fitness newsletter** — often $50–200 for a mention to a
  few thousand engaged people. Search "home gym newsletter sponsorship".
- **Micro-influencers** — home gym accounts with 5k–50k followers will often
  post for $50–150, or for free equipment. DM ten, expect two replies.

# The first two weeks, concretely

**Day 1** — Push the ASIN fix live. Modmail the r/homegym mods. Set up the
Pinterest business account and claim the domain. Make TikTok / Instagram /
YouTube profiles with `gymplanner.org` in each bio.

**Days 2–7** — Comment genuinely on r/homegym and 3 Facebook groups daily, no
links at all. Pin 3–5 Pinterest pins a day. Record and post the first 3D video.

**Days 8–14** — Start answering "will this fit" posts with plan links. Keep
pinning. Post 3 more videos. Watch `/ops` for where traffic actually comes from.

**What success looks like at day 14:** a few hundred visitors and, critically,
**3 qualifying Amazon sales**. That is the number that keeps the account alive
and proves the model works. Revenue at this stage will be small — likely under
$100 — and that is fine and expected. You are buying proof, not income.

# What will actually kill this

- **Posting affiliate links on social.** Bans you, and loses the Amazon account.
- **Spamming Reddit.** Gets `gymplanner.org` domain-banned sitewide, permanently.
  This is the worst outcome available to you and it is easy to trigger by
  accident.
- **Not finishing the Amazon tax interview.** Commissions are withheld until
  it's done. You already know about this one — do it.
- **Dead product links.** We just fixed six. If a buyer's cart comes back half
  empty, they don't come back. Re-check monthly.
